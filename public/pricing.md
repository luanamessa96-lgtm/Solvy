# Solvy — Pricing

## Free plan
- Price: €0, free forever
- Credit card: not required
- No trial period — the free plan does not expire
- Features:
  - Income and expense tracking
  - Real-time dashboard
  - Tax estimate (IVA / IRPF)

## Pro Monthly
- Price: €14.99/month
- Billing: monthly, cancel anytime
- Everything in the free plan, plus:
  - Unlimited invoices
  - Corrective invoices (facturas rectificativas)
  - Modelo 303 and Modelo 130
  - Deadline alerts for every fiscal date
  - Receipt OCR
  - Libro registro / invoice backup as PDF and ZIP ("Archivo Solvy")
  - Multiple fiscal profiles

## Pro Yearly
- Price: €149.90/year (equivalent to €12.49/month)
- Saving: 17% vs monthly plan — 2 months free
- Billing: annual, one payment
- Features: same as Pro Monthly

## Notes
- All plans cover Spain (autónomos, Estimación Directa Simplificada, RETA) and Italy (Regime Forfettario / Ordinario)
- Solvy does not file your returns and does not give tax advice — that stays with you or your gestor. Solvy keeps your data in order and gives you day-to-day visibility.
- VeriFactu integration is in development — not available yet. It will ship before the obligation starts for autónomos (July 2027).
- Payments handled by Stripe
- Prices include VAT where applicable
